After this email + preview flow is set, you should expect this:

A prospect goes to `platos.agency`, fills out the brief, gets a branded email, signs into Plato’s Core, and sees a **preview workspace built from their own ICP**. They do not get full leads yet. The whole experience should make them think, “Okay, this is real. I want to see this turned on for my market.”

What happens next in the flow:

```text
platos.agency brief
→ Core webhook creates workspace
→ GPT parses ICP/sources/filters
→ branded email sends signup link
→ user signs in with same email
→ preview workspace loads
→ CTA pushes activation call
→ you sell DIY signals or managed outreach
```

To launch ASAP, do this in order:

1. **Finish Core preview gate**
   Make sure users only see preview data before activation.

2. **Wire website to Core**
   The agency site must POST to:
   ```text
   https://plato-redesign.replit.app/api/webhooks/brief
   ```

3. **Test the full flow with your own email**
   Fill out the live site form, get the email, sign in, confirm your preview appears.

4. **Create the activation call script**
   Simple sales flow:
   ```text
   Here’s the workspace your brief created.
   Here’s your ICP.
   Here are the sources we recommend.
   Here’s what live sourcing unlocks.
   Here’s what we can deliver weekly.
   Do you want DIY signals or managed outreach?
   ```

5. **Pick your first launch offer**
   I’d keep it simple:
   ```text
   $1,500 setup
   $1,000-$2,500/month for signal delivery
   Managed outreach upsell: +$1,500-$3,500/month
   ```

6. **Launch to warm network first**
   Don’t mass launch yet. Send to 10-20 people/agencies/founders you know and say:
   ```text
   I built a tool that creates a signal workspace from your ICP and shows where active buyer intent may be hiding. Want me to generate a preview for your business?
   ```

7. **Use the demo as the sales call**
   Best close:
   ```text
   This is what your preview found.
   Activation turns on live sourcing, routing, and delivery.
   ```

Once the full test works, you are launchable. Not “perfect SaaS launch” launchable, but **sell-first managed service launchable**, which is what we want.
